System you want to explore *

On-Grid

Best for reducing electricity bills

On-Grid solar uses the utility grid and can export surplus energy through net metering. It usually has a lower upfront cost because it does not include batteries, but it normally switches off during a grid outage for safety.

Off-Grid

Best for backup power or limited grid access

Off-Grid solar combines solar panels and batteries to power selected appliances during outages or where grid access is limited. It requires critical-load and backup-hour inputs, and typically costs more upfront because battery storage is included.
