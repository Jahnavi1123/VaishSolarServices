On-Grid Solar System

An on-grid solar power system is connected to the utility grid. The electricity it produces is routed to the grid and used to run appliances, making installation fuss-free and maintenance simple.

How does an on-grid solar power system work?

Solar modules absorb sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC). A solar inverter changes the DC into alternating current (AC) to power electrical items. The electricity is then routed to the grid for day-to-day use. A net meter records energy supplied to the grid and energy consumed; the monthly bill reflects the outstanding balance.

Benefits of on-grid solar

  1. Zero electricity bills: You pay only for surplus electricity consumed. When you use less than your system generates, the excess can be fed back to the grid.
  2. Easy maintenance: Fewer components and no batteries make the system simple to install and maintain.
  3. Passive income generation: Surplus electricity sent to the grid can earn credits, reducing bills and creating value from excess generation.
02

Off-Grid Solar System

Also known as a stand-alone power system (SAPS), an off-grid system generates electricity from solar panels and stores it in solar batteries through a charge controller. An inverter then powers home or business appliances, including at night or during lower sun exposure.

Components of an off-grid system

  • Solar panels: The array size and output depend on available sunlight, usable roof space, and energy-consumption needs.
  • Solar batteries: Storage keeps the home running after sunset by saving excess power generated during the day.
  • Solar inverter: A stand-alone inverter converts solar DC into AC for most household appliances and electronics.
  • Solar charge controller: Regulates voltage and current to protect batteries from overcharging and damage.
  • Alternative energy source: A backup generator can support the system during periods of very low solar production.

Advantages of off-grid solar

  1. No utility-grid access required: In remote areas, off-grid solar can be more affordable than extending power lines.
  2. Energy self-sufficiency: Become independent of electricity retailers and rising power prices while remaining protected from grid failures and blackouts.
03

Hybrid Solar System

Hybrid solar systems combine the strengths of grid-tied and off-grid systems. They can be described as off-grid solar with utility backup power, or grid-tied solar with additional battery storage.

Advantages of hybrid solar

  1. Lower cost than off-grid: Hybrid systems generally need a smaller battery bank and no backup generator. Off-peak utility electricity can be less expensive than diesel.
  2. Smart solar potential: Modern inverters can respond to changing utility rates. You can use lower-cost power off-peak, store excess solar energy, and export it when grid rates are highest.

Equipment for hybrid solar systems

  • Charge controller
  • Battery bank
  • Additional DC disconnect
  • Battery-based grid-tie inverter
  • Power meter

Battery-based grid-tie inverter

These devices draw power to and from battery banks while synchronizing with the utility grid. For most homeowners, using the utility grid for backup electricity remains more practical and economical than relying only on battery banks or generators.

Solar LED Street Light

A solar lamp, also known as a solar light or solar lantern, is a lighting system made up of an LED lamp, solar panels, battery, charge controller, and sometimes an inverter. The lamp operates using electricity stored in batteries charged through solar photovoltaic panels.

All-in-One Solar Light

All-in-one solar street lights are compact, new-age lighting solutions that integrate solar panels, an LED light, a lithium-ion battery, and a PIR motion sensor. They use high-quality materials and are designed for Indian environments.

Solar Water Heating System

Solar water heating converts sunlight into heat for water heating using a solar thermal collector. We provide direct, indirect, and pressurized heating systems for domestic and commercial use, reducing the need for electricity to produce hot water.

How do solar water heating systems work?

Heat is transferred from the collector to the tank through a separate circuit and heat exchanger. The panel heats water, oil, or another fluid flowing through pipes into a copper coil inside the hot-water tank.

Types of solar water heating systems

  • Flat Plate Collector Technology
  • Evacuated Glass Tube Technology
  • Heat Pipe Technology

Solar Water Pumping System & Solar Atta Chakki

Solar water pumping systems support irrigation, drinking-water, and other water requirements. The pump runs on electricity generated by solar panels, making operation economical and environmentally friendly. A typical system includes a solar charge controller, customised pump, and solar PV module.

Types of solar water pumping systems

  1. Solar submersible pumps
  2. Solar open-well pumps
  3. Solar monoblock pumps
  4. Solar AC water pumping systems

Major applications

  • Domestic drinking water
  • Industrial water requirements
  • Irrigation
  • Hospitals, schools, colleges, and universities

Key features

  • Eco-friendly model
  • Economical water pumping system
  • Inbuilt protection system
  • 2-year warranty

Solar Atta Chakki

Solar Atta Chakki is especially useful in villages, where it can reduce running costs. Traditional Atta Chakki units often run on diesel, which becomes more expensive over time. A one-time solar project investment provides long-term benefits with minimal maintenance.
